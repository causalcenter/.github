# Center for Dynamic Causality

The Center for Dynamic Causality is an independent research organization devoted to the study of **dynamic causality**. The Center studies three research areas: Dynamic Causality, Dynamic Chronometry, and Dynamic Knowledge.

## Research Areas

**1. Dynamic Causality** — materialized as [DeepCausality](https://deepcausality.com), the reference implementation of the [Effect Propagation Process (EPP)](https://github.com/deepcausality-rs/deep_causality/tree/main/papers). DeepCausality subsumes Pearl's SCMs, Granger causality, dynamic Bayesian networks, the Rubin Causal Model, and CATE inference as parametric specializations, and is [hosted at the Linux Foundation for Data & AI](https://lfaidata.foundation/projects/deepcausality/).

**2. Dynamic Chronometry** — a foundational experiment that uses DeepCausality to invert the Einstein Field Equation under the weak-field limit, recovering the geocentric gravitational constant *GM* from Galileo satellite atomic-clock data to a median precision of 4 × 10⁻⁷. Source code: [chronodynamics](https://github.com/causalcenter/chronodynamics); reference dataset: [Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20020236).

**3. Dynamic Knowledge** — pursued under the working name [Deep Brain](https://github.com/causalcenter/deep_brain), the epistemic counterpart of the EPP, exploring how a knowledge corpus can be represented as a continuously evolving process rather than a static snapshot.

## Open by Default

Foundational papers as preprints, production-quality Rust crates under the MIT license, and reference datasets with persistent DOIs. Collaborate via the [DeepCausality repository](https://github.com/deepcausality-rs/deep_causality) or the [Discord community](https://discord.gg/Bxj9P7JXSj).
